One Hundred Scores

Well-known public-domain music, fed to the my-game-audio engine as the sheet music it was written in. There are no recordings or samples: code computes every note, deterministically, from the score. Engine .

    Every score is in the public domain or under CC0: OpenScore's orchestral editions (via the Hauptstimme corpus), CC0 transcriptions from PDMX (the Public Domain MusicXML dataset, from MuseScore), and the Mutopia Project's public-domain engravings. Each piece's source and licence are listed in library/README.md.

    The small number beside a piece is Meta's Audiobox Aesthetics score for its first minute: a learned guess at how much a listener enjoys it, from 1 to 10. It stands in for ears; yours are the better judge.
